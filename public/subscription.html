Subscription Policy

Effective date: 24 August 2026  ·  ProgressOr  ·  progressor.tracker@gmail.com
ProgressOr operates on a simple monthly subscription with no hidden fees, no auto-renewal, and no lock-in. You pay when you choose to.

The subscription grants full access to all features of the Service including project boards, dashboards, Jira/GitLab/GitHub integrations, sprint tracking, and notes.

02 Billing & Payment

  • Payment processor: all payments are handled securely by Ameriabank vPOS. We never store your full card details.
  • Accepted methods: Visa and Mastercard debit/credit cards.
  • Currency: AMD (Armenian Dram). The price is shown in AMD at checkout.
  • Receipt: a payment receipt is emailed to you automatically after each successful payment, with a PDF attached.
  • Failed payments: if a payment fails, your subscription is not activated. Try again with a different card or contact your bank.

03 Access & Expiry

  • Your subscription starts immediately upon successful payment and lasts for 30 days (or the period shown at checkout).
  • You will receive an email reminder 1 day before expiry with a direct link to renew.
  • When your subscription expires, you lose access to the application but your data is retained for 30 days. If you renew within that window, all your data is restored exactly as you left it.
  • After 30 days of no renewal, we may permanently delete your account data. We will notify you before doing so.

04 Renewal

To renew, log in to ProgressOr, open your profile, and click Renew subscription. You can also renew directly from the reminder email we send before expiry.

Renewals extend your access by one month from the renewal date, not from the original expiry date. Early renewals stack — renewing before expiry adds time on top of your remaining days.

05 Price Changes

We may change the subscription price at any time. We will notify existing users by email at least 30 days before a price change takes effect. You can renew at the current price before the change date if you wish to lock it in for an additional month.

06 Admin-Granted Subscriptions

In some cases, ProgressOr may grant subscription access manually (e.g. for trials, partnerships, or support resolutions). Such grants are provided at our discretion and are not subject to the refund policy.
