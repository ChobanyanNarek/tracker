Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 August 2026  ·  ProgressOr  ·  progressor.tracker@gmail.com
ProgressOr is a project management tracker. We collect only what is necessary to provide the service, we do not sell your data, and you can request deletion at any time.

This Privacy Policy describes how ProgressOr ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects personal information when you use our web application at progressor.work ("the Service"). By using the Service you agree to the practices described here.

We comply with Armenian data protection law and, where applicable, the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

01 Data We Collect

We collect the following categories of information:

  • Account data: your first name, last name, email address, and phone number — provided when you register.
  • Authentication data: hashed passwords and session tokens stored server-side; auth tokens stored in your browser's local storage.
  • Subscription & payment data: subscription status, subscription expiry date, and payment records (order ID, masked card number last 4 digits, amount, currency, transaction timestamps). Full card data is processed exclusively by Ameriabank vPOS and never stored by us.
  • Application state: the project boards, tasks, developer entries, sprint data, notes, and schedule you create within the Service — stored server-side as a JSON blob linked to your account.
  • Integration credentials: API tokens and configuration you provide for Jira, GitLab, and GitHub connections — stored encrypted at rest.
  • Usage data: server access logs (IP address, browser user-agent, timestamp, endpoint) retained for security and debugging.

We do not use tracking cookies, advertising pixels, or third-party analytics scripts.

02 How We Use Your Data

  • To create and manage your account and authenticate you.
  • To process subscription payments via Ameriabank vPOS.
  • To store and sync your project data across devices.
  • To send transactional emails: email verification codes, payment receipts, and subscription expiry reminders.
  • To connect to Jira, GitLab, and GitHub on your behalf using the credentials you provide.
  • To maintain security, diagnose errors, and improve the Service.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose not listed above.

03 Data Sharing & Third Parties

We share data only as necessary to operate the Service:

  • Ameriabank vPOS — processes payment card transactions. Governed by Ameriabank's own privacy policy.
  • Render.com — cloud infrastructure hosting our backend and PostgreSQL database (EU/US data centers).
  • Google (Gmail SMTP) — used to send transactional emails from progressor.tracker@gmail.com.
  • Atlassian / GitLab / GitHub — data is fetched from these services using your own credentials when you trigger a sync. We do not share your data with them beyond what you initiate.

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal data to any third party.

04 Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active. If you request account deletion, we will delete your personal data within 30 days, except where retention is required by law (e.g. financial records).

Server access logs are retained for up to 90 days.

05 Security

We protect your data using industry-standard measures: TLS encryption in transit, encrypted storage at rest, hashed passwords, and access controls. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure; we cannot guarantee absolute security but commit to prompt notification if a breach affects your data.

06 Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Delete your account and associated data.
  • Export your application data (available via the Export function in the Service).
  • Object to processing or withdraw consent.
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority (EU users: your national DPA).

To exercise any of these rights, email us at progressor.tracker@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

07 International Data Transfers

Our infrastructure is hosted on Render.com, which may process data in the United States or European Union. Where data is transferred outside the EEA, we rely on standard contractual clauses or equivalent safeguards.

08 Children

The Service is not directed at individuals under 16 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it promptly.

09 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. We will notify registered users by email at least 14 days before material changes take effect. Continued use of the Service after the effective date constitutes acceptance.
