Refund Policy

Effective date: 24 August 2026  ·  ProgressOr  ·  progressor.tracker@gmail.com
If you are not satisfied with ProgressOr, you can request a full refund within 7 days of your payment. No lengthy forms — just email us with your order ID.

01 Eligibility

You are eligible for a refund if all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your refund request is made within 7 calendar days of the payment date.
  • You have not made extensive use of the Service during that period (e.g. the account has not been actively used for project tracking across multiple sessions).
  • This is your first refund request for this account.

Partial refunds (e.g. for unused days remaining in a subscription period) are not available. Refunds are for the full payment amount or not at all.

02 How to Request a Refund

  • Email us at progressor.tracker@gmail.com with the subject line: Refund Request.
  • Include your order ID — this is the transaction reference shown in your payment receipt email.
  • Briefly describe why you are requesting a refund (optional, but helpful for us to improve the product).
  • We will review your request within 2 business days and reply with a decision.

03 Refund Timeline

Once a refund is approved:

  • The refund is processed back to the original payment card via Ameriabank vPOS.
  • Processing typically takes 5–10 business days to appear on your statement, depending on your bank.
  • Your subscription access is revoked immediately upon approval.

We will send you a confirmation email once the refund has been initiated.

04 Exclusions

Refunds will not be issued in the following cases:

  • The 7-day window has passed since the payment date.
  • The account showed significant active use during the refund window.
  • The subscription was granted by an administrator (not purchased directly).
  • The request appears to be abusive or fraudulent (e.g. repeated refund requests, chargebacks filed simultaneously).
  • The account was suspended or terminated due to a violation of the Terms of Service.

We reserve the right to decline refund requests at our discretion in exceptional circumstances, with a written explanation provided.

05 Chargebacks

If you initiate a chargeback with your bank before contacting us, we may be unable to process a voluntary refund. Please reach out to us first — we respond within 2 business days and will resolve legitimate issues promptly.
